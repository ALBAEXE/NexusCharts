# v1.4.0 — Daily close at 5 pm New York
### Changed
- Daily candles for gold, metals, energy, Forex and indices now close at 5 pm New York time, as with brokers. Sunday evening is part of Monday's candle.
- The live daily candle switches to the new day right at the 5 pm close.
- Crypto keeps closing at midnight UTC; stocks follow the New York session.
- Data sources explains the daily close; history before 12 November 2026 still closes at midnight UTC.
