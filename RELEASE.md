# v1.14.0 — Strategy Tester
### Added
- Strategy Tester: a full-screen page with the results of a strategy, in three tabs.
  - Overview: net profit, closed trades, percent profitable, profit factor, max drawdown, average trade and the equity curve.
  - Performance: the same figures split into All, Long and Short.
  - Trades: every trade, most recent first, with entry, exit, price, quantity, profit and cumulative profit; open trades are marked.
- Next to a strategy's name on the chart, a small tag shows its net profit; tap it to open the Strategy Tester. Indicators that are not strategies are unchanged.

### Changed
- strategy.netprofit now counts closed trades only, as in Pine Script.

### Documentation
- README updated in 5 languages: new in 1.14.0.
