# v1.21.0 — Volume and levels rebuilt
### Added
- On Balance Volume with smoothing and another timeframe; Volume with colour based on previous close, colours, volume MA (type and length) and a volume-spike alert.
- Pivot Points High Low with separate left/right bars and colours for highs and lows, optional prices and lines, and alerts.
- ZigZag with price deviation, depth, line colour and width, extend to last bar, and labels with reversal price, price change (absolute or percent) and cumulative volume.
- New: Pivot Points Standard with Traditional, Fibonacci, Woodie, Classic, DM and Camarilla levels (P, R1–R5, S1–S5) on automatic, daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly or yearly pivots, with labels and prices.
- Levels: previous day/week high, low, close and midpoint with price labels and alerts; daily, weekly and monthly opens; ADR in price or percent with today's range and alert; change % against day, week, month or year; historical volatility with days per year; N-week high/low with labels and alerts; session high/low for the whole day or a custom session and time zone.
- All indicators of the library have now been rebuilt.
